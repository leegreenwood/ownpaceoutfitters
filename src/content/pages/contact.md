## Schools and DofE centres

If you're a school, youth organisation, or DofE centre looking for an expedition provider — for one award level or a full Bronze-to-Gold programme — get in touch and we'll talk through your group's needs, dates, and what a season with us would look like.

**Email:** [hello@ownpaceoutfitters.co.uk](mailto:hello@ownpaceoutfitters.co.uk)

## General enquiries

Parents, participants, or anyone with a general question about Own Pace Outfitters are welcome to reach out through the same address — just let us know that up front so we can point you in the right direction.

Own Pace Outfitters is a trading name of Independent Youth Volunteer Support (IYVS) CIC. For questions about IYVS itself, or its other programmes, visit [IYVS](https://iyvs.org.uk), [Zanshin Archery](https://zanshinarchery.co.uk), or [Greenwood Bushcraft](https://greenwoodbushcraft.co.uk).
